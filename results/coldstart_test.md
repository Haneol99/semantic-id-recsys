# Cold-start evaluation — test split, `data/processed_coldstart` (paper Sec. 4.3)

415 held-out (unseen) items = 5% of distinct test targets; 1,098 of 22,363 test users have an unseen target. eps = maximum share of unseen items in the top K, as unseen slots = ceil(eps·K) (primary). Mean [95% bootstrap CI over users]; differences paired, **bold** = CI excludes 0.

- **TIGER** (paper method, primary): beam search (30) over all items' Semantic IDs; seen items by exact 4-code match, unseen items by first-3-code match, at most `unseen slots` of them.
- **TIGER (exact-scored unseen)** (labeled variant, not the paper's method): TIGER's beam seen items in the first K − slots places, the unseen items with the highest exact full TIGER log-prob in the last slots.
- **Hybrid**: SASRec-CE (seed 42) top seen items + Semantic-KNN's top unseen items in the last slots (eps 0 = plain SASRec-CE).
- **Semantic-KNN**: cosine to the mean Sentence-T5 embedding of the last 1 history items (n picked on valid Recall@10: {1: 0.0503, 2: 0.0491, 3: 0.045, 5: 0.0421, 10: 0.0398}); all items ranked, same cap as TIGER.
- TIGER's beam generated an unseen item's ID in 0.0000% of beam slots; short lists at eps 0: 20.
